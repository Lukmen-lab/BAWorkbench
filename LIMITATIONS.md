# Scientific and technical limitations

See [DISCLAIMER.md](DISCLAIMER.md) for the full assumption-of-risk and no-liability notice. Lukmen Lab and contributors do not assume responsibility for use of this software or its outputs.

- BAWorkbench enumerates hypotheses; it does not demonstrate biosynthesis, stability, abundance, or biological activity.
- Reaction rules are structure-edit models, not kinetic or thermodynamic simulations.
- Plausibility categories and warning flags require expert review.
- The 7-oxo core definitions retain source/stereochemistry review notes and should be independently verified for production analyses.
- Glycoside anomeric configuration and polymer-fragment representations are model assumptions.
- A generated RDKit conformer may not represent a relevant bound conformation, especially for large or flexible ligands.
- Exact masses and adducts aid prioritization but do not replace authentic standards or MS/MS confirmation.
- The software has not been validated for clinical or diagnostic use.
