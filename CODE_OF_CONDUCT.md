# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and professional project community.

Participants must communicate constructively, respect differing experience and perspectives, avoid harassment and discriminatory behavior, and focus criticism on ideas and evidence. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions or restrict participation when behavior is unsafe, abusive, or persistently disruptive.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the contact mechanisms listed on the Lukmen-lab GitHub organization page.
